Traffic Violation Detection Dataset

This project uses a custom dataset created by combining and preprocessing multiple traffic violation datasets.

Primary Dataset:
https://universe.roboflow.com/sample2-yj0ke/violation-l6kaz

The dataset was further cleaned, merged, balanced, and annotated for training the final YOLOv8 model.

Note:
The complete dataset (~680 MB) is not included in this repository because of its size.
